# Random Video Transcript Summary

## Goal
Open a random video in a different Chrome window and summarize it from its transcript.

## Verified test
Video: Computer Science Vs Software Engineering | How to Pick the Right Major

The workflow successfully:
- Opened the video in a separate Chrome window.
- Verified the new Chrome target.
- Retrieved a 16,422-character transcript.
- Summarized the video's comparison of computer science and software engineering.

## Summary of the verified video
Computer science is presented as the more theoretical/mathematical field, covering areas such as algorithms, data structures, computation, AI, cryptography, and research. Software engineering applies many of those CS ideas to designing and maintaining large real-world software systems, where teams need requirements, planning, architecture, lifecycle management, and structured development processes.

## Risk / Stability
Low risk. It only opens public YouTube content and reads available captions.
