# HTZ Stock Review Ability

## Goal
Provide a concise fundamental review of Hertz Global Holdings (NASDAQ: HTZ).

## Result
HTZ was classified as a speculative turnaround / watchlist stock rather than a core holding.

## Key evidence
- Q2 2026 revenue approximately $2.4B, up 10% year over year.
- Adjusted Corporate EBITDA $81M, improving $63M year over year.
- Adjusted net loss remained $47M.
- High leverage, negative equity and weak free cash flow remain the primary risks.

## Risk / Stability
High risk. Hertz remains highly sensitive to financing costs, fleet depreciation, used-car values and execution. The turnaround is showing measurable operating progress, but balance-sheet risk remains significant.

## Sources reviewed
Hertz Q2 2026 earnings release and SEC filing, plus recent analyst rating and target data current through August 2026.
